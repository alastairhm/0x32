Since it’s holiday season already, let’s keep the tech jokes coming with this parody of a famous song entitled "The Twelve Days of Christmas." You can sing along if you like:<br /><br />The 12 Bugs of Software<br /><br />For the first bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the second bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"Ask them how they did it and<br />See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the third bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"Try to reproduce it,<br />Ask them how they did it and<br />See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the fourth bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"Run with the debugger,<br />Try to reproduce it,<br />Ask them how they did it and<br />See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the fifth bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"Ask for a dump,<br />Run with the debugger,<br />Try to reproduce it,<br />Ask them how they did it and<br />See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the sixth bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br />"Reinstall the software,<br />Ask for a dump,<br />Run with the debugger,<br />Try to reproduce it,<br />Ask them how they did it and<br />See if they can do it again."<br /><br />For the seventh bug of Christmas, my manager said to say:<br
