Following on from [Al's Fractals](/posts/als-fractals-comes-home/), the second of my old Blogger blogs is now folded into this one: [Geek Monkey](https://meekgonkey.blogspot.com/), 218 posts of "geeky or techie things I've found interesting" between 2008 and 2017.

Where Al's Fractals was mostly my own renders, Geek Monkey was more of a linkblog — Linux distros, Raspberry Pi and Arduino projects, browser oddities, a lot of "10 free things you should bookmark" roundups, some of it written, a lot of it reposted via feed aggregators from sites that don't exist anymore. That last part made this import a bit more interesting than the last one.

Same mechanics as before — Google Takeout's Atom export, parsed with the original titles, HTML bodies, labels, and publish timestamps intact — but this time I also ran a full link-rot pass over all 2,205 outbound links across the 218 posts. **54% of them are dead.** Not randomly either: it clusters almost entirely around infrastructure that stopped existing years ago — FeedBurner's proxy domains, Gawker Media's syndication tier (gone in its 2019 bankruptcy), AOL's old blog network, a defunct ad service called Pheedo, Yahoo Pipes. My favourite find was `hongki.at`, a broken image-tracking proxy baked into every reposted article's footer, whose own 404 placeholder images — filed helpfully under `/images/404/`— are now themselves 404. A dead link about being a dead link.

Three posts came out the other side with *no* surviving links at all and got dropped, same call as the Photobucket posts last time. The other 101 affected posts kept their content but got a note added up top flagging how many of their links are broken and why. The remaining 215 are tagged `geek` and `blog-import`, and landed as drafts for a read-through before going live — some of this holds up better than other bits of it.

Two down, however many more Blogger blogs are still sitting in that takeout export to go.
