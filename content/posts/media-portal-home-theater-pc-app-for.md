And this week the developers decided the product was stable enough to remove the beta label and <a href="http://www.team-mediaportal.com/news/global/mediaportal_1.0_final_released.html">launch Media Portal 1.0</a>.<br /><br />The application was initially based on the popular <a href="http://xbmc.org/">XBMC</a> application designed to turn Xbox gaming consoles into media playback workhorses. But Media Portal quickly outgrew its beginnings as a Windows port of XBMC. Version 1.0 is a full featured application for accessing music, movies, pictures, weather, and other information from a full screen interface. If you have a TV tuner in your computer you can also use Media Portal to watch and record live TV.<br /><br />I just installed Media Portal 1.0 on my netbook, and aside from the fact that I had to download and install Microsoft's .NET framework first, the process was pretty much a breeze. There's a one-click install option that lets you select common settings. Or more advanced users can configure the application to their heart's content.<br /><br />The team is now turning its attention to <a href="http://www.team-mediaportal.com/news/global/announcement_mediaportal_2.html">Media Portal 2</a>, which will be more modular and will may eventually incorporate new capabilities like the ability to stream media across a home network or over the internet to portable devices like cellphones.
