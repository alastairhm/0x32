Something I've been meaning to do for years, finally done: [Al's Fractals](https://alsfractals.blogspot.com/), my old fractal art blog on Blogger, now lives here too.

It started back in March 2008, long before this blog existed, as a dumping ground for whatever I'd rendered that week — [gnofract4d](https://fract4d.github.io/gnofract4d/) parameter files, [Ultra Fractal](https://www.ultrafractal.com/) exports, [Context Free](https://www.contextfreeart.org/) grammars, the occasional [XaoS](https://xaos-project.github.io/) zoom. It ran on and off for about a decade, drifting later on into IFTTT reposts of fractal art I'd stumbled across on Pinterest, before quietly going dormant.

Bringing it over turned out to be refreshingly mechanical. Google Takeout exports Blogger blogs as an Atom feed with every post's original title, HTML body, labels, and — crucially — its original publish timestamp, so nothing needed backdating by hand. A [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) session parsed the export, converted 148 posts into Hugo front matter, and mapped Blogger's labels onto tags. 31 of those turned out to embed images hosted on Photobucket, which killed free hotlinking [years ago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photobucket#2017_hotlinking_controversy) — those got dropped rather than published broken. The remaining 117 are live now, spanning 2008 to 2017, each carrying its real publish date and tagged `blogger-import` and `fractal` so they're easy to find as a set.

It's a fun read back, if you're into that sort of nostalgia. Software I'd completely forgotten existed, a lot of enthusiasm for the maths of pretty pictures, and a reminder that this is at least the third or fourth blog I've run over the years — [0x32](/) is just the one that stuck.
