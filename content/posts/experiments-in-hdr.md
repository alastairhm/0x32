Here is my first real attempt at an HDR image, taken from three shot with a 1.3+- exposure bracketing.<br />The foreground is a bit blurry as the wind was moving the plants and I wasn't using a tripod, notes for next time.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/doodle_m/4776720007/" title="Ripon Sunset HDR by Alastair Montgomery, on Flickr"><img src="http://farm5.static.flickr.com/4102/4776720007_2035ce11c9.jpg" width="500" height="342" alt="Ripon Sunset HDR" /></a><br /><br /><ul><br /><li>After converting from the RAW files I arranged the three images as separate layers in Photoshop.<br /><li>The layers where arranged as +1.3 exposure,normal,-1.3.<br /><li>Then I added layer masks to the top two layers.<br /><li>Painting on the top layer mask I removed the "burnt out" sections from the top layers so the correctly exposed section from the layer below showed through.<br /><li>I repeated this again on the second layer.<br /><li>I ran a low Gaussian blur on the layer masks to soften the edges of the masks to reduce the halo effect.<br /><li>Then I merge each layer into the one below to create the finished image.<br /></ul><br /><br /><p style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-size:85%;"><b>Alastair Montgomery's Photography</b></span></p><p style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-size:85%;"><a href="http://doodlem.blogspot.com/">Main Page</a> <a href="http://www.zazzle.com/doodle_m*">My Shop at Zazzle</a> <a href="http://sicklittlemonkey.deviantart.com/gallery/">DeviantArt Account</a> <a href="http://www.redbubble.com/people/Alastair">Red Bubble Account</a></span></p><p style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-size:85%;"><a href="http://www.redbubble.com/people/Alastair"></a><a href="http://www.zazzle.com/?rf=238269554763928881"><img src="http://i186.photobucket.com/albums/x259/alastair_hm/imagehost/home_10.gif" width="141" height="57" alt="In association with Zazzle.com" /></a></span></p>
