After my first week with my 32gb Nexus 5 here are some initial thoughts compared to my previous phone Samsung Galaxy S2. Nothing major to complain about.<br />
<br />
<u>Likes</u><br />
<ul style="text-align: left;">
<li>Camera App and its better integration with Google Plus and on phone picture editing.</li>
<li>The screen is very good.</li>
<li>Feels very fast, good response in switching apps, swiping between screens and scrolling.</li>
</ul>
<br />
<u>Dislikes</u><br />
<ul style="text-align: left;">
<li>You can assign custom ringtones from Google Play Music, had to resort to a third party app to get ringtones assigned.</li>
<li>Alarm has some functionality removed like the smart alarm "soft" wake up.</li>
</ul>
